# ADR-013 — validation criteria and result records without an evaluator (2026-10-19)
- Status: Proposed; awaiting owner review of the implementing task (Task 2B).
- Decision: extend the dependency-free domain layer (ADR-011, ADR-012) with `domain.validation`.
  1. Versioned `ValidationCriteria` (`CriteriaId` plus a non-blank version) hold at least one `TargetCondition` and one `ImprovementCondition`, plus zero or more guards (`AbsoluteGuard`, `DeltaGuard`). Condition IDs are unique across all three collections. Every condition names an exact metric version and analysis scope (`MetricSelector`) and uses typed enums (`ComparisonOperator` LT/LE/GT/GE, `ImprovementDirection`, `BaselineReference`). Thresholds and deltas are `MeasuredScalar` values with explicit units. The minimum improvement must be strictly positive (absolute change from the pre-attempt state, not a percentage); the maximum guard delta must be non-negative.
  2. `ValidationResult` records a supplied verdict (`ACCEPTED`/`REJECTED`/`INCONCLUSIVE`), a `TargetStatus` (`SATISFIED`/`UNMET`/`UNKNOWN`), an `AssessmentMode` (`INITIAL`/`ATTEMPT`) and one `CheckResult` per condition (`PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNAVAILABLE`/`NOT_APPLICABLE`). PASS and FAIL require evidence references. NOT_APPLICABLE means outside the assessment mode, not missing evidence.
  3. Acceptance rule for ATTEMPT assessments (design validation rules 1–5): all required evidence is available, every guard passes, and either the targets are SATISFIED or the targets are UNMET and every improvement condition passes (retained intermediate improvement). A failing improvement check therefore does not prevent acceptance when targets are SATISFIED. An ACCEPTED INITIAL assessment requires SATISFIED targets, passing absolute guards and NOT_APPLICABLE improvement and delta-guard checks.
  4. Consistency is checked in two places. Construction rejects combinations that are contradictory without the criteria: ACCEPTED with UNKNOWN target status, ACCEPTED with an UNAVAILABLE check, and an ACCEPTED INITIAL assessment that is not SATISFIED or contains a FAIL check. It does not judge FAIL checks in ATTEMPT mode, because acceptance depends on check categories it cannot see. `validate_validation_references` checks against the criteria: identity and version, exactly one check per condition, mode applicability (INITIAL defers improvement and delta-guard checks; ATTEMPT has no NOT_APPLICABLE), acceptance consistency by category (no failing guard under ACCEPTED; every improvement passing for ACCEPTED with UNMET targets), and target-status consistency (SATISFIED: every target passes; UNMET: at least one target fails; UNKNOWN: at least one target unavailable and none failing).
  5. No evaluator: verdicts are not computed, thresholds are not compared, metrics and observations are not resolved, and no default conditions, thresholds or units exist.
- Source: owner's Task 2B instruction of 2026-10-19; mixing-core design, validation rules 1–6.
- Rationale: fixed, versioned, machine-checkable criteria and results support declared-in-advance accept/revert decisions (FR-10), regression and side-effect comparison (FR-09) and complete run records (FR-12, QR-08). These are deterministic, offline-testable records (QR-02). Separating record consistency from verdict computation keeps scientific choices (metrics, thresholds, precedence, evidence compatibility) out of the vocabulary until they are justified.
- Clarification history: the original Task 2B instruction listed "ACCEPTED with any FAIL check" as contradictory, which conflicts with accepted intermediate improvement (UNMET requires a failing target). The first implementation allowed FAIL under ACCEPTED only on target checks of an UNMET result. The owner confirmed on 2026-10-19 that the instruction was contradictory and corrected the rule to item 3: failing improvement checks are compatible with ACCEPTED when targets are SATISFIED, and the blanket constructor rejection of FAIL checks based on target status was removed.
- Consequences:
  - Groundwork only for FR-09, FR-10, FR-12, QR-02 and QR-08; none is met. Full verdict precedence, evidence compatibility (scope, alignment, analysis and render settings) and threshold justification belong to a later evaluator task and to calibration (SR-02, methodology).
  - Unit compatibility with a metric catalog remains open. Selector scopes are not checked against a project snapshot here.
  - Guards may be empty structurally; whether configurations must require them is a later decision.
